# Organise your bookshelf by author and series

Choose Author or Series in the bookshelf Sort by menu. Books in a series sort by numeric reading order, including 2.5 between 2 and 3. Sort choice is remembered. Search now finds authors and series as well as titles.

Select a book and choose Edit book details to enter or correct its author, series and book number. New EPUB imports read embedded author and supported Calibre/EPUB 3 series metadata; Word imports read their author. Existing books can be organised manually. Corrections survive reopening a book. Metadata stays local.

Author and series details appear on cover cards and in List view. Saved listening positions, bookmarks and Continue reading are preserved.
